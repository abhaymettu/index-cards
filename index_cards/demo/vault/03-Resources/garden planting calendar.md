# garden planting calendar

Zone ~7b-ish, last frost around mid April, first frost around late Oct/early Nov. Dates are rough. Adjust by actual soil temp, not the calendar.

## Late winter (Feb–Mar)

- Start indoors: onions, leeks, celery (slow, start early), peppers (late Feb), tomatoes (mid-late March)
- Peppers need heat mat. Germination is miserable without it
- Direct sow when soil is workable: peas, spinach, radish. Peas go in as soon as I can get a fork in the ground
- Order seed potatoes by end of Feb or the good varieties sell out
- Check old seed packets. Onion and parsnip seed are bad after 1 yr, toss

## Spring (Apr–May)

- Early April: potatoes, kale, lettuce, chard, beets, carrots (direct)
- Mid April: broccoli and cabbage transplants. Cover if frost threat
- Tomatoes, peppers, eggplant out only after nights stay above 50F. Usually ~May 10. Harden off 7-10 days, no skipping this
- Mid May: beans, cucumbers, squash, corn. Soil should be 65F+ or seeds just rot
- Basil after tomatoes, it hates cold
- Succession sow lettuce and radish every 2 weeks until it gets hot

## Summer (Jun–Aug)

- June: second round of beans, last chance for summer squash
- Mid July: start fall brassicas indoors (broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower). Shade them, too hot outside
- Early Aug: direct sow carrots and beets for fall
- Late Aug: lettuce, spinach, arugula again once it cools a bit
- Water deep 1-2x a week rather than daily sprinkling
- Pull garlic scapes in June, harvest garlic when bottom 3 leaves brown (usually early July)

## Fall (Sep–Nov)

- Sept: transplant fall brassicas, sow spinach, mizuna, radish
- Mid Oct: plant garlic. Roughly 2 weeks after first hard frost is fine, just get it in before ground freezes. Mulch heavy
- Cover crop (rye or clover) on empty beds by early Oct
- Pull tomato vines before they rot, don't compost diseased ones
- Collect and dry seed from beans and lettuce

## Notes to self

- Rotate: don't put tomatoes/peppers/potatoes where nightshades were last year
- Keep a log of actual first/last frost dates. Mine is off by about a week from the charts
- Too many zucchini last year. Two plants max
- Mark the garlic bed location, I forgot twice
