# meditation log

## current setup
- 10 min, timer on phone, airplane mode so nothing buzzes mid-sit
- same spot: floor cushion by the window, back against nothing. chair works too if knees are bad that day
- morning before coffee is the only slot that sticks. after lunch I just doze
- bell sound at the end, not at the start. starting bell made me tense up

## technique notes
- counting breaths 1–10 then reset works best when I'm wound up. if I hit 10 without drifting, I'm probably zoning out, not focused
- noting ("thinking", "planning", "itch") helps more than trying to blank the mind
- body scan top-down is good for tired days, but I fall asleep if lying down. sit up
- when stuck on a bug from the day before, I let myself write one line on paper first, then sit. otherwise the whole 10 min is debugging
- eyes half open, soft gaze at the floor. fully closed = sleepy or spinny

## what derails me
- screens right before. even checking one message ruins it
- too much caffeine the day prior, restless legs and racing thoughts
- skipping two days in a row, third day feels like starting over
- trying to "do it right". the sit is the sit

## log
- week 1: 10 min x 5 days, mostly planning loops, nothing profound
- week 2: missed 3 days, travel. did 5 min on the plane, counted breaths, fine
- week 3: noticed I clench my jaw and shoulders when thinking about deadlines. relax on exhale helps
- week 4: first sit where the gap between thoughts felt noticeable. lasted maybe 20 sec
- week 5: 15 min trial, too long, back to 10
- week 6: steady, 6 of 7 days. more patient in standup-type situations, less snappy over small stuff
