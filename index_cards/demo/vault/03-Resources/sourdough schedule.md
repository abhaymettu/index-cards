# sourdough schedule

Working template for a weekend loaf. Adjust times backward from when I actually want to eat bread. Kitchen is ~70°F most of the year, colder in winter, so bulk runs longer then.

## Base recipe (1 loaf)

- 500g bread flour (sometimes 50g swapped for whole wheat)
- 375g water (75% hydration, bump to 78% once handling feels easy)
- 100g active starter
- 10g salt

Starter should be fed 4-6 hrs before mixing and be doubled, domey, and floats in water. If it sinks, wait another hour or feed again.

## Timeline

**Friday night**
- 9:00 pm: mix flour + water, rest (autolyse) 30-60 min
- 9:45 pm: add starter and salt, squish in by hand
- 10:00 pm: first fold in the bowl

**Saturday**
- Folds every 30 min x4 over the first 2 hrs
- Bulk ferment 4-6 hrs total at room temp, until ~50-75% bigger with bubbles at the edges
- Pre-shape, rest 20 min, final shape
- Into banneton seam-up, cover, straight to fridge
- Cold proof 12-16 hrs (up to 24 is fine, just a bit more sour)

**Sunday**
- Preheat oven and Dutch oven to 475°F for a full 45 min
- Score cold dough straight from fridge
- Bake covered 20 min at 450°F, then uncovered 20-25 min until deep brown
- Cool at least 1 hr before cutting. Don't skip this, crumb is gummy otherwise

## Troubleshooting

- **Dense, tight crumb:** underfermented. Let bulk go longer or warm the dough.
- **Flat, spreads out:** overproofed or shaping too loose. Shorten the cold proof or build more tension.
- **Pale crust:** bake uncovered longer, or oven wasn't hot enough.
- **Too sour:** shorter cold proof, feed starter more often.
- **Dough sticks to banneton:** rice flour dusting, not just AP.

## Notes to self

- Winter: put dough in the oven with only the light on, ~78°F, bulk drops to ~4 hrs
- Use a clear straight-sided container for bulk so rise is easy to eyeball, rubber band at the starting level
- Keep a small log of times and results, helps more than guessing
- Leftover starter discard goes into pancakes or crackers, don't toss it
- If plans change, dough can wait in the fridge after the first few folds, shape the next morning
