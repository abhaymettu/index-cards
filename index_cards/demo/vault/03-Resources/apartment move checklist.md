# apartment move checklist

## 6–8 weeks out
- give notice to landlord in writing, keep a copy + timestamp of the email. check lease for exact notice window, mine was 60 days
- book movers NOW, month-end slots go first. get 3 quotes, ask if stairs/elevator/parking permit are extra
- start the "do I still need this" pass: closet, desk drawers, cables box. sell/donate anything not used in a year
- ask current ISP about transfer vs cancel. new place may only have one provider, check availability by address before committing
- order boxes + tape in bulk. wardrobe boxes for coats are worth it

## 2 weeks out
- change address: bank, cards, employer/payroll, insurance, car registration, subscriptions, USPS forward (takes a few days to kick in)
- schedule internet install for the *day before* move-in if possible, installers get backed up
- reserve elevator / loading dock at both buildings
- photograph everything in current place (walls, floors, appliances) for the deposit dispute I might have
- pack non-essentials, label by room + a rough priority (1 = open first)
- defrost freezer, use up pantry stuff

## Packing notes
- keep monitors in original boxes if I still have them, otherwise wrap in towels and mark FRAGILE on 2 sides
- label cable bags by device, take a phone pic of the back of the desk setup before unplugging
- backup laptop before move. don't pack it, carry it
- essentials bag: chargers, meds, toiletries, towel, shower curtain, toilet paper, box cutter, basic tools, bedsheets
- heavy stuff (books) in small boxes only, learned this the hard way
- disassemble bed frame, tape hardware to the frame itself in a ziploc

## Move day
- do a walkthrough of the new place BEFORE unloading, note damage with photos
- keep cash for tipping movers
- parking: cones or a friend saving the spot
- last sweep of old place: closets, cabinets, behind doors, balcony, storage unit
- meter readings (electric/gas) with a photo at both places
- return keys, get written confirmation of handover

## After
- test every outlet, faucet, smoke detector, and the heat/AC right away, report issues in writing within the first few days
- update address with the remaining stuff: voter registration, license, gym, packages/online shopping accounts
- unpack kitchen + bed + bathroom first, everything else can wait a week
- chase the deposit return, lease usually says 30 days
- break down boxes and list them for free pickup before they pile up
