# marathon training plan

16 weeks, 4 runs/wk + 1 optional cross-train. Peak ~45 mi. Goal is finish strong, not a PR. If a week goes sideways, don't try to "make up" miles, just move on.

## weekly shape

- Mon: off (or yoga, 20 min max)
- Tue: easy 5-6 mi, strides at the end if legs feel ok
- Wed: cross-train / bike, skip if tired
- Thu: workout day. tempo or intervals, 6-8 mi total w/ warmup/cooldown
- Fri: off
- Sat: easy 4 mi shakeout OR rest the day before long run if sleep was bad
- Sun: long run

## long run progression

- wks 1-4: 8, 10, 12, 8 (cutback)
- wks 5-8: 13, 15, 16, 12
- wks 9-12: 18, 19, 20, 14
- wks 13-14: 20 (last big one), 16
- wks 15-16: taper. 12, then 8, then race

Every 4th week is a cutback. Don't skip those even when feeling great, that's when I get dumb and hurt.

## pacing

- easy = conversational, should be able to talk in full sentences. Usually 1:30-2:00/mi slower than goal pace. Be honest about this, I always run easy days too fast.
- tempo = comfortably hard, ~15-20 sec/mi faster than goal pace
- long run: first half easy, last 3-4 mi at goal pace on wks 9+ only
- HR cap on easy days: 145ish

## fueling + gear

- gel every 40 min after the first 45 min. Practice with the exact brand being used on race day.
- water at every aid station, electrolyte tabs in the bottle on runs over 14 mi
- carb load 2 days out, not 3, 3 felt bloated last time
- NEW SHOES: break in by wk 6, rotate two pairs, retire at ~400 mi
- nothing new on race day. Seriously. Not socks, not shorts.

## watch for

- shin tightness = drop a run, not push through
- if resting HR is up 8+ bpm for 3 days, take an extra off day
- sleep < 6.5 hrs two nights in a row, swap workout for an easy run
- foam roll + hip stuff after Thu and Sun at least

## taper notes

Cut volume, keep a little intensity. Expect to feel sluggish and weirdly anxious around wk 15. That's normal. Lay out race kit the night before and do a 2 mi shakeout the day before.
