# reading list 2026

## how i'm doing it this year
- cap at 2 books in flight. 3 was too many last year, half-finished everything
- one technical, one not. alternate so my brain doesn't fry
- 30 min before bed, phone in the other room. this is the only thing that actually worked
- if not hooked by page 50, drop it. no guilt, just move on
- paper for anything with diagrams, kindle for the rest

## finished so far (rough ratings)
- distributed systems one, the long one: 4/5. chapters on replication and consistency worth rereading. skipped the last third
- a short book on writing clearly: 5/5. changed how I write PR descriptions
- sci-fi trilogy part 1: 3/5. great setup, slow middle
- database internals book: 4/5, dense. took 3 months because I kept stopping to try things in a scratch repo
- a memoir about burnout: 3/5. useful in spots, a bit repetitive

## in progress
- performance engineering book, around 40%. the profiling chapters are the good part, the tooling sections are already dated
- a history of the early internet, slow but fun. reading ~10 pages a night

## lessons / notes to self
- reading with a project beats reading cold. I retained way more from the db book because I was building alongside it
- take notes only on stuff I'd actually look up later. otherwise it's just transcription
- highlight on kindle, then dump highlights into the vault once a month, not per-book
- library hold queue is faster than buying, check there first
- audiobooks don't work for technical stuff, fine for memoir and history
- don't buy new books while the queue is >5. I keep breaking this
