# Lantern-Maps

## Overview
Lantern-Maps is a small mapping tool for plotting and sharing low-light walking routes, mostly for night commutes and evening runs. Idea is to take open map data, score streets by lighting and foot traffic, then spit out a safer route instead of just the shortest one. Keeping v1 narrow: route scoring, a simple API, a basic viewer. No accounts, no mobile app yet. Main risk is patchy lighting data, so I need a fallback heuristic for areas with no coverage.
