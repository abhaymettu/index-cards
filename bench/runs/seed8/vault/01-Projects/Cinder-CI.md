# Cinder-CI

## Overview
Cinder-CI is a lightweight CI runner I want for small side projects where the big hosted pipelines feel like overkill. Main idea: run builds in throwaway containers, cache deps aggressively, and keep config to one short file. Goal is sub-minute feedback on typical repos. Not trying to replace anything heavy, just something fast I can self-host and actually understand end to end. Need to figure out log streaming early since that's probably the messy part.
