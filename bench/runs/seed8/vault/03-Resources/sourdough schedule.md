# sourdough schedule

Baseline for a weekend loaf, ~24h start to finish. Adjust by kitchen temp, not by the clock.

## Ingredients (one big boule)

- 450g bread flour, 50g whole wheat
- 375g water (75%), warm-ish
- 100g active starter (fed 4-6h before, should be doubled and domed)
- 10g salt

Cold kitchen? Bump water to ~80F and expect +1-2h on bulk. Hot kitchen, cut starter to 60-70g.

## Timeline

- **Fri 8pm**: feed starter, 1:5:5, leave on counter overnight if it's under 70F. Otherwise fridge it for a few hours first.
- **Sat 8am**: mix flour + water, autolyse 45 min.
- **Sat 9am**: add starter + salt, squish in with wet hands. Rest.
- **9:30 to 12:30**: 3-4 stretch and folds, every 30 min. Stop when dough feels airy and holds shape.
- **~1pm**: bulk should be 50-75% risen with bubbles at edges. Don't wait for double, that's how I get flat loaves.
- **1:30pm**: preshape, bench rest 20 min, final shape, into floured banneton seam up.
- **2pm**: into fridge, cold proof 12-16h.
- **Sun morning**: preheat dutch oven at 500F for a full 45 min.

## Bake

- Score straight from fridge, cold dough scores cleaner.
- Lid on 20 min at 475F, then lid off 20-25 min at 450F.
- Internal temp ~205-210F. Deep brown is good, pale is underbaked.
- Cool at least 1h. Cutting early = gummy crumb, every time.

## Troubleshooting

- **Dense, tight crumb**: underfermented. Longer bulk or warmer water next time.
- **Flat, spread out**: overproofed, or shaping too loose. Shorten cold proof.
- **Gummy**: cut too soon, or oven not hot enough.
- **Sour too mild**: longer cold proof, up to 18h. Slightly more whole wheat helps too.
- **Sour too sharp**: shorten the fridge time, feed starter more often.

## Misc

- Poke test: dent springs back slowly = ready. Springs back fast = wait. Doesn't spring = overdone, bake now.
- Rice flour in the banneton stops sticking way better than AP.
- Parchment sling makes dutch oven loading less scary.
- Keep a rubber band on the jar to track starter rise.
- Discard goes in the fridge for pancakes or crackers, don't toss it.
