# meditation log

## what's working
- 10 min, right after coffee, before opening laptop. Anything later in the day just doesn't happen.
- Timer with soft bell, no guided audio. Guided stuff makes me passive.
- Sitting on the folded blanket on the floor > chair. Chair = slouch = asleep by minute 6.
- Counting breaths 1 to 10, restart on drift. Dumb but it keeps me honest. If I hit 10 without losing count, that's a good sit.
- Phone on airplane mode in the other room. Not just silent. Silent doesn't count.

## what's not
- Evening sits. Brain is still in work mode, just replays standup and review comments for 10 min.
- Trying to "clear my mind." Wrong goal. Noticing the drift is the actual rep.
- Skipping a day and then "making up" with 20 min. Ends up resentful. Just do 10 the next day.
- Sitting right after a heavy lunch. Nope.

## patterns I've noticed
- Bad sleep (<6h) → more fidgety, but the sit helps more than on good-sleep days. Do it anyway.
- Debugging something stubborn the night before = I'll spend the sit mentally rubber-ducking. Fine. When it happens, let it run for a minute, then come back to breath.
- Jaw and shoulders are where I carry stuff. Quick scan at minute 2 and drop them. Usually they're up near my ears.
- Around minute 7 is the wall. Urge to check the timer. Just ride it out, minute 8 is usually calmer.

## log format
Keeping it minimal so I don't abandon it:
- date | minutes | quality (1-3) | one-line note
- Example: `Oct 28 | 10 | 2 | restless, shoulders tight, finished anyway`
- Don't rate quality on how calm it felt. Rate on whether I showed up and noticed drift.

## streak stuff
- Current streak ~3 weeks. Not tracking the number obsessively, just a checkbox in the daily note.
- Missed days: no guilt, just restart the count. Streak anxiety defeats the purpose.
- Maybe try 15 min once the 10 feels boring. Not yet.
