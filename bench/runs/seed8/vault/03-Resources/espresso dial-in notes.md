# espresso dial-in notes

Baseline recipe I keep coming back to: 18g in, 36-40g out, 25-30s, water ~93C. Start here every new bag, then adjust one thing at a time. Changing two variables at once = wasted shots.

## Reading the shot

- Sour, thin, sharp, finishes fast → under-extracted. Grind finer or raise the yield a bit.
- Bitter, dry, ashy, harsh finish → over-extracted. Grind coarser or cut the time.
- Tastes hollow but not sour → probably channeling, not grind. Check puck for holes/cracks after.
- Gushing in <18s is almost always too coarse or a dosing issue, not the beans.
- Choking / dripping past 40s: go coarser, but check basket isn't overdosed first.

## Order of adjustments

1. Grind size (biggest lever, tiny steps, maybe 1-2 clicks)
2. Yield (ratio) if the taste is close but balance is off
3. Temp (only after grind and ratio settled; lighter roasts like hotter, darker roasts like cooler)
4. Dose last, it shifts everything

Always purge a few grams after changing grind. Leftover grounds in the burrs throw off the next shot, wasted two shots on this last week.

## Puck prep

- Weigh the dose to 0.1g, don't eyeball it.
- WDT with a thin needle tool, break up clumps, especially with static.
- Level, tamp straight, firm but not heroic. Consistency > pressure.
- Spritz a drop of water on beans before grinding if static is bad (RDT).
- Wipe the basket dry between shots. Wet basket = uneven flow.

## Beans / freshness

- Best window is roughly 7-30 days off roast. Too fresh = lots of CO2, gassy and sputtery, bloom in the puck.
- Past ~5 weeks, flavor flattens, need to grind finer to compensate.
- Store airtight, dark, room temp. Freezing in single-dose portions works fine, grind straight from frozen.
- Dark roasts: shorter ratio (1:1.5-1:2). Light roasts: longer (1:2.5+) and finer.

## Maintenance

- Backflush with water every day, detergent weekly.
- Brush the grinder every few days, deep clean burrs monthly.
- Descale on a schedule based on water hardness, roughly every 2-3 months here.
- Swap the group gasket when it starts leaking at the edges or shots get inconsistent.

## Log template

`date | bean | days off roast | dose | grind | yield | time | notes (taste)`

Keep writing the taste note even if it's just "ok". The pattern shows up after ~10 entries.
