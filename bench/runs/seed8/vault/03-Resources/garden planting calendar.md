# garden planting calendar

Zone 7ish, last frost around Apr 15, first frost around Oct 25. Adjust by a week either way depending on the spring. Check the soil thermometer, not the calendar.

## Late winter (Feb–Mar)

- Start onions, leeks, and celery indoors early Feb. Slow starters.
- Peppers indoors ~8 wks before last frost, so mid Feb. Heat mat helps a lot.
- Tomatoes indoors early-mid March. Don't start too early, they get leggy and sulk.
- Direct sow peas as soon as soil is workable (mid March). Soak overnight.
- Order seed potatoes by end of Feb.

## Spring (Apr–May)

- Early April: spinach, lettuce, radish, kale, carrots direct sow. Cold tolerant.
- Plant potatoes ~2 wks before last frost.
- Harden off seedlings 7 days before transplant. Skipped this once, lost half the peppers to sun scald.
- Tomatoes out after Apr 20 if nights stay above 50F. Bury stems deep.
- Early May: beans, cucumbers, squash, corn direct sow once soil hits 60F.
- Basil outside only when nights are reliably warm, mid May. It blackens at the first chill.

## Summer (Jun–Aug)

- Succession sow beans and lettuce every 2-3 wks through July.
- Mid July: start fall brassicas (broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower) indoors or in a shaded bed.
- Early Aug: sow carrots and beets for fall harvest.
- Garlic bed: clear it after harvest in July, cover crop or mulch.
- Water deep, twice a week, rather than a little daily.

## Fall (Sep–Nov)

- Late Aug to mid Sep: spinach, lettuce, radish, arugula for fall.
- Transplant fall brassicas by early Sept.
- Plant garlic mid-late October, cloves pointy end up, 2 in deep, mulch with straw.
- Pull tomatoes and peppers before first hard frost, or cover on cold nights to squeeze out a few more weeks.
- Leaf mulch on empty beds. Compost whatever is left.

## Notes for next year

- Tomatoes went out too early this year, a cold snap set them back. Wait.
- Plant fewer zucchini. Two plants is plenty.
- Rotate beds: nightshades did not go where they were this year.
- Keep a simple log of actual sow dates and first harvest dates, so this calendar can be tuned.
- Label everything. Unlabeled rows are a guessing game by June.
