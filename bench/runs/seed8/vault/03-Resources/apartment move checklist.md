# apartment move checklist

Reference for next time. Stuff I forgot or almost forgot last move.

## 6-8 weeks out

- Give notice to landlord in writing, keep a copy. Check lease for how many days required (mine was 60, nearly missed it)
- Book movers or truck NOW. End of month slots go first. Get quote in writing, ask about stair/elevator fees
- Start purging. Anything I haven't touched in a year goes to donate or sell
- Ask new building about elevator reservation + move-in hours. Some require a certificate of insurance
- Photograph current unit fully (walls, floors, appliances) for deposit dispute later

## 2-3 weeks out

- Internet: schedule install at new place BEFORE moving. Wait times can be 2 weeks+. Cancel/transfer old one for the day after move-out
- Transfer or set up electric, gas, water. Need start date and old stop date
- Change address: bank, credit cards, employer/payroll, insurance, USPS forwarding, DMV, subscriptions, package accounts
- Order boxes, tape, wrap. Wardrobe boxes for clothes saves tons of time
- Defrost freezer, use up food. Stop buying groceries about 10 days out
- Back up laptop + NAS before anything gets packed. Label all cables with tape as they come off

## Packing notes

- Label by room AND priority (open first / can wait)
- Essentials box: toiletries, chargers, shower curtain, toilet paper, scissors, box cutter, basic tools, bedding, coffee stuff
- Keep monitors in original boxes if I still have them, otherwise lots of padding + "fragile" on all sides
- Take photo of back of router/desk setup before unplugging
- Heavy stuff (books) in small boxes only. Learned this the hard way
- Pack valuables, passport, laptop, meds myself. Not on the truck

## Moving day

- Walk through with phone camera before locking up: closets, cabinets, outlets
- Meter readings photo at old place (electric, gas)
- Return all keys, fobs, garage remotes. Get receipt or email confirmation
- Tip movers in cash, have water on hand
- Check new unit for damage and note it in writing same day

## After

- Test smoke detectors, find breaker box + water shutoff first night
- Change locks or ask management if rekeyed
- Confirm deposit return timeline, follow up if it passes
- Unpack kitchen + bedroom first, everything else can wait a week
- Update address on anything that bounced from the first pass
