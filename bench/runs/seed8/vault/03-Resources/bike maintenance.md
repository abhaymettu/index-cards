# bike maintenance

## after every ride
- wipe chain w/ rag, quick look for grit stuck in the links
- squeeze tires, check for embedded glass/thorns. pull them out before they work in
- spin wheels, watch for rub on brake pads or frame
- if wet ride: dry the chain, relube next morning (not same night, lube needs to sit)

## weekly-ish
- tire pressure. road ~85-95 psi at my weight, commuter tires ~65. lose a few psi/week even w/o a leak
- lube chain: one drop per roller, wipe off excess. excess just collects dirt
- check quick release / thru-axle tight
- bolts on stem and seat post, finger-snug check, torque only if loose

## monthly
- degrease drivetrain properly: chain, cassette, chainring. old toothbrush for cassette gaps
- check brake pad wear lines. replace when the groove is basically gone
- inspect cables/housing for fraying at the ends
- chain wear check w/ checker tool. swap at 0.5% on the tool, don't wait for 0.75 or it eats the cassette
- wipe rims/rotors w/ isopropyl. never lube near rotors

## torque reference
- stem faceplate bolts: 5 Nm
- seat post clamp: 5-6 Nm (carbon post: check the printed max, use paste)
- crank bolts: ~40 Nm
- rotor bolts: 6 Nm
- cleat bolts: 5-6 Nm, grease threads
- always grease threads on steel into alu, anti-seize on seat post

## seasonal / yearly
- full tune up before spring: new cables + housing, bar tape, bleed hydraulic brakes
- replace tires when the center is flat or cracks show on sidewall
- repack hub and headset bearings if they feel gritty or notchy
- sealant top up every 3-4 months if running tubeless (it dries into a booger)
- winter: fenders on, heavier wet lube, rinse off road salt asap

## flats
- carry: spare tube, 2 levers, mini pump or CO2, patch kit, multitool
- check inside the tire casing with fingers before putting the new tube in
- pinch flats = pressure too low. snakebite holes come in pairs
- tubeless plug for small cuts, tube as backup if the sidewall is torn
