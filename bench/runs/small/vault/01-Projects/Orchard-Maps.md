# Orchard-Maps

## Overview
Orchard-Maps is a native iOS app for mapping fruit trees and orchard plots. Idea is to let growers drop pins on individual trees, tag variety and planting year, and log things like pruning, spraying, and harvest yield. Offline-first since cell signal in the rows is bad, syncs when back on wifi. Want a simple map layer showing which blocks are due for work. Keeping scope small for v1: pins, tags, notes, offline storage. Reporting and export can wait.
