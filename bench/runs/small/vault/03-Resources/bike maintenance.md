# bike maintenance

## chain
- wipe + relube every ~150 km, or after any wet ride. don't wait for the squeak
- wet lube for winter, dry lube for summer/dusty. don't mix w/o degreasing first
- apply 1 drop per link, spin cranks backwards, wipe excess. excess just collects grit
- check wear w/ chain checker at 0.5%. swap chain then, otherwise cassette dies too (~2000-3000 km per chain)
- quick link pliers live in the saddle bag

## tires
- road: 80-90 psi, commuting on rougher stuff drop to ~70. check before every ride, they lose a few psi a week
- gravel/commute tires: 45-55 psi depending on load
- look for embedded glass after rides, pick it out w/ a pin before it works through
- rear wears faster, rotate front<->rear only if wear is even (usually isn't)
- tubeless sealant: top up every 3-4 months, it dries into a booger

## brakes
- pads: replace when groove is gone or <1mm material left
- squealing = contaminated or glazed. sand pads lightly + wipe rotor w/ isopropyl. never lube near rotors
- bleed hydraulic every ~year. DOT fluid vs mineral oil, CHECK which before buying (mixing ruins seals)
- lever pulls to bar = air in the line, bleed

## drivetrain tweaks
- skipping under load = worn chain or stretched cable
- barrel adjuster: quarter turn at a time, shifts late → turn out (counterclockwise), shifts early → turn in
- hanger bent? sight down from behind the wheel. cheap to replace, carry a spare

## torque numbers (bolts that matter)
- stem faceplate: 5 Nm
- seatpost clamp: 5-6 Nm (carbon: use paste, stay low)
- pedals: 35 Nm, left pedal is reverse thread
- crank bolt: ~50 Nm
- thru axle: 12 Nm
- use a torque wrench on carbon parts, no exceptions

## schedule
- every ride: tire pressure, quick bolt check, brakes squeeze test
- monthly: clean + lube chain, inspect cables, wipe frame
- every 6 months: full wash, check bearings (headset play, wheel hubs), tape/grips
- yearly: brake bleed, cables/housing, bottom bracket check
- end of season: deep clean, fresh sealant, store somewhere dry, hang or keep tires inflated

## kit
- multitool, 2 tubes (even tubeless), levers, mini pump, patch kit, quick link, zip ties, tiny bit of duct tape wrapped on pump
