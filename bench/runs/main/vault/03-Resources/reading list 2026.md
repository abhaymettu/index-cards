# reading list 2026

## how i'm tracking things
- one running list here, finished stuff gets a rating out of 5 and one line of why
- cap "currently reading" at 3. more than that and nothing gets finished
- fiction before bed, technical in the morning. tried reversing it, bad idea
- if i'm not into it by 50 pages, drop it. no guilt. 50 pages rule has saved me a ton of time this year
