# garden planting calendar

Zone 7ish, last frost around Apr 15, first frost around Oct 28. Adjust if spring runs weird. Check the thermometer in the soil, not the calendar.

## Late winter (Feb–Mar)

- Start onions and leeks indoors, early Feb. Slow, need ~10 weeks.
- Peppers indoors mid Feb. They sulk if started late.
- Tomatoes indoors ~6-7 weeks before last frost, so around Mar 1. Don't start earlier, they get leggy and I run out of window space.
- Direct sow peas as soon as soil is workable. Mid Mar usually fine.
- Order seed potatoes by end of Feb.

## Spring (Apr–May)

- Potatoes in ground early Apr, soil ~45F.
- Direct sow: spinach, radish, carrots, beets, lettuce. Succession sow lettuce and radish every 2 weeks.
- Brassicas (broccoli, cabbage) transplant early Apr, cover with row fabric for cabbage moths.
- After last frost (late Apr): tomatoes, peppers, basil out. Harden off 5-7 days first. Basil needs nights above 50F or it blackens.
- Early May: beans, cucumbers, squash, corn direct sow. Soil needs ~60F.
- Don't plant squash before May 10. Lost a whole round once to a cold snap.

## Summer (Jun–Aug)

- Second sow of bush beans mid Jun and again mid Jul.
- Start fall brassicas indoors mid Jul, transplant early Aug.
- Late Jul: carrots and beets for fall, keep the bed damp until they sprout (burlap or board over the row helps).
- Stop pinching basil flowers by Aug, let some go for the bees.
- Water deeply 1-2x a week, not daily sprinkles.

## Fall (Sep–Nov)

- Sow spinach, lettuce, radish early Sep.
- Garlic: plant mid Oct to early Nov, after first hard frost is fine. Mulch 4 inches of straw.
- Pull tomatoes and peppers when frost hits. Green tomatoes ripen in a paper bag.
- Cover crop (rye or clover) on empty beds by early Oct. Late is better than never.
- Clean and oil tools, drain hoses before freeze.

## Notes to self

- Take photos of the beds each month, I forget what went where.
- Rotate beds: no nightshades in the same spot two years running.
- Compost top-dress in March and again after summer crops come out.
- Keep a running list of what actually worked in a separate note, this calendar is the plan, not the result.
