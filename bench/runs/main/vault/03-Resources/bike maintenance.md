# bike maintenance

## chain
- lube every ~150 km or after any ride in rain. wipe chain first w/ rag, then drip 1 per link, wait few min, wipe off excess. excess = grit magnet
- degrease + deep clean every ~800 km, sooner if gritty. old toothbrush works fine
- check wear w/ chain checker. replace at 0.5% on 11/12sp, 0.75% on lower speeds. waiting too long eats cassette + chainring
- quick link pliers live in the toolbox, reuse quick links max once

## tires
- road: 80-90 psi, rear a bit higher. drop ~10 for wet
- check pressure before every ride, loses 5-10 psi a day
- look for embedded glass/thorns after each ride, pick out w/ pin
- rear wears faster, swap front to rear when center is flat, then new one on front
- spare tube + mini pump + 2 levers + patch kit always in saddle bag

## brakes
- pads: replace when wear line gone or <1 mm. squealing = glazed or contaminated, sand lightly w/ fine paper
- rim brakes: wipe rims w/ isopropyl, toe-in pads slightly
- disc: never touch rotor w/ greasy fingers. bleed every 1-2 yrs or if lever feels spongy
- bed in new pads: 10 hard stops from moderate speed

## drivetrain misc
- skipping under load usually = stretched chain or worn cassette, not indexing. try new chain first
- ghost shifting / slow shifts: cable barrel adjuster, quarter turn at a time
- derailleur hanger bends easily, keep spare hanger for the frame

## bolts + torque
- stem faceplate bolts ~5 Nm, alternate in X pattern
- seatpost clamp ~5-6 Nm, carbon paste on carbon parts
- pedals: right is normal thread, left is reverse. grease threads
- check crank bolts + cleat bolts monthly

## schedule
- weekly: tire pressure, quick bolt check, wipe down
- monthly: clean + lube chain properly, check brake pads, cables
- every 6 mo: wheel true check, bar tape, hub feel for play
- yearly: full service (bearings, cables + housing, brake fluid if disc)
