# marathon training plan

18 weeks, 4 runs/wk + 1 optional cross-train. Goal: finish comfortably under 4:15, not blow up after 30k.

## weekly shape

- Mon: rest or 30 min easy spin
- Tue: easy 8-10k + strides (6x20s)
- Wed: quality — tempo or intervals
- Thu: off, mobility 15 min
- Fri: easy 6-8k
- Sat: long run, the one that matters
- Sun: recovery jog 5k or full rest if legs feel dead

Don't stack hard days. If Wed wrecks me, Fri gets cut to 30 min, no guilt.

## paces (update after time trial)

- easy: 6:20-6:45/km, should be able to talk in full sentences
- tempo: ~5:35/km
- marathon pace: ~5:50/km
- intervals (1k reps): ~5:05/km w/ 90s jog rest

Easy days are almost always too fast. Check HR, keep under ~145. Walk the hills if needed.

## long run progression

- start at 14k wk 1, add ~2k per week
- every 4th week is a cut-back (-25%)
- peak 32k around wk 14, two of those max
- last 8-10k of 3 long runs at marathon pace
- taper: wk 16 at 75%, wk 17 at 50%, race week just shakeouts

## fueling

- gel every 40 min starting at 40 min, practice on every long run over 18k
- water at every aid station, electrolytes tab in the bottle
- breakfast 2.5 hrs before: oats, banana, coffee. Don't change this on race day
- stomach issues last time from the sweet gels, stick with the citrus ones

## gear / body notes

- rotate two pairs of shoes, retire the old ones at ~700k
- left calf tightens when mileage jumps >10% wk to wk, so don't
- foam roll calves and glutes after long runs
- sleep 7.5+ hrs on the two nights before quality sessions
- vaseline on nipples and inner thighs, every long run, no exceptions
