# tax paperwork checklist

## docs to gather (do this by end of Jan)
- W-2s from every employer, incl. the short stint one that's easy to forget
- 1099-NEC / 1099-K for any freelance or side gigs
- 1099-INT and 1099-DIV from bank + brokerage (brokerage ones often arrive late Feb, sometimes corrected in March, so don't file too early)
- 1099-B / cost basis report if I sold any stock or RSUs
- 1098 mortgage interest, property tax statement
- 1095-A if on marketplace insurance
- HSA statement (5498-SA / 1099-SA)
- Student loan interest 1098-E
- last year's return, need AGI for e-file verification

## equity comp stuff
- RSU vest: check that the W-2 already includes the income. Basis on 1099-B is often wrong (shows $0 or missing the vest value), so adjust on Form 8949
- ESPP: need the purchase date, FMV on purchase, discount. Qualifying vs disqualifying disposition changes how it's reported
- keep the vest confirmations PDFs in one folder, don't rely on the broker portal keeping them forever

## deductions / credits to remember
- home office only if self-employed, W-2 employees can't claim it
- HSA contributions made outside payroll
- charitable receipts, anything over $250 needs written acknowledgment
- estimated tax payments made during the year (dates + amounts from bank statements)
- retirement contributions, IRA deadline is the filing deadline, not Dec 31
- state: check if any credits differ from federal

## filing notes
- compare the software's refund against last year as a sanity check, big swings = something missing
- e-file + direct deposit is fastest
- save PDF of the filed return and the confirmation email in the tax folder
- estimated payments for next year: Apr, Jun, Sep, Jan. Put reminders on calendar right after filing
- keep records at least 3 years, 7 if anything involves a loss or bad debt claim
