# apartment move checklist

## 6 weeks out
- give notice to landlord in writing, keep a copy. check lease for the notice window (usually 30 or 60 days), don't assume
- book movers or truck now, weekends and end of month fill up fast
- start the "use it up" pile: pantry, freezer, cleaning stuff. stop buying bulk anything
- measure new place: doorways, elevator, where the desk and couch actually go. sketch rough layout
- ask new building about elevator reservation / loading dock rules. some need a deposit + COI from movers

## 2 weeks out
- change address: bank, cards, employer/payroll, insurance, car registration, subscriptions, phone plan
- USPS forwarding (set start date, takes a few days to kick in)
- schedule internet install at new place. ask for a date BEFORE move day, installs slip constantly
- transfer or open electric/gas, cancel old ones effective move-out date
- boxes: get small ones for books, big for light stuff. label by room + "open first"
- take photos of old place, all walls, floors, appliances, for deposit dispute later

## Week of
- defrost freezer, empty fridge a day early
- pack a "day one" bag: chargers, laptop, meds, toiletries, towel, shower curtain, toilet paper, box cutter, basic tools, bedsheets
- back up laptop + take photos of how cables are plugged in behind the desk/tv
- disassemble furniture, bag screws and tape the bag to the piece
- confirm movers the day before, get a cash tip ready
- keep important docs (passport, lease, checks) on me, not in the truck

## Move day
- walk old place with phone video before leaving, check closets and drawers
- return keys, get written confirmation or take a photo of the handoff
- meter readings, photo of each (gas, electric)
- at new place: check for damage first, photo anything pre-existing, email landlord same day
- set up bed and desk first, everything else can wait

## After
- ask for deposit return timeline, follow up if past the legal deadline
- recycle boxes / post on local free group, they go in a day
- test smoke detectors, find water shutoff and breaker panel
- update address on anything missed (the stragglers show up over the next month)
- fix wifi placement, router location matters more than I think
