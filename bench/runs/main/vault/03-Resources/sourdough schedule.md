# sourdough schedule

Baseline for a weekend loaf, started Friday night, baked Saturday. Adjust if kitchen is cold (<20°C) or hot (>26°C).

## Timeline

- Fri ~9pm: feed starter. 1:5:5 (starter:flour:water) if I want it peaked by morning, 1:2:2 if the kitchen is warm
- Sat 8am: starter should be doubled, domed, bubbly. Float test is unreliable for me, go by the look and smell
- Sat 9am: mix. 500g bread flour, 375g water (75%), 100g starter, 10g salt. Autolyse flour+water 30 min first, then add starter and salt
- 9:30–12:30: bulk. Stretch and folds at 30, 60, 90 min. Four sets is overkill, three is fine
- ~12:30: bulk done when ~50% bigger, jiggly, edges domed. Don't wait for doubling, that's how I get flat loaves
- 12:45: preshape, rest 20 min, final shape
- 1:15pm: into banneton, seam up, straight into fridge
- Sun or Sat evening: bake cold from fridge (anywhere from 8 to 20 hrs is fine)

## Bake

- Preheat dutch oven at 250°C for a full 45 min, not 20
- Score about 1cm deep, one confident slash
- Lid on 20 min at 240°C, lid off 20–25 more at 220°C
- Internal temp ~96°C when done. Deep brown is better than pale, always
- Cool at least 1 hr before cutting. Yes, really

## Troubleshooting

- Dense, gummy crumb: underproofed, extend bulk by 30–45 min next time
- Flat and spread out: overproofed or weak starter. Check starter first
- Pale crust: oven not hot enough or lid off too early
- Sour too mild: longer fridge retard, up to 24 hrs
- Sour too sharp: shorter retard, use less starter and feed it more recently
- Dough sticking during shaping: wet hands, not more flour

## Notes

- Warm kitchen: cut bulk to ~3 hrs total and use cooler water (~22°C)
- Cold kitchen: put dough in the oven with only the light on, that's about 26°C
- Whole wheat swap: up to 20% is fine with no other changes, more than that needs extra 25g water
- Discard starter goes into pancakes or crackers, don't toss it
- Keep starter in the fridge between bakes, feed weekly, take it out 2 days before a bake and feed twice
