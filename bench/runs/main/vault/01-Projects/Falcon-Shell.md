# Falcon-Shell

## Overview
Small, fast interactive shell I want for my own daily use. Main goals: sane scripting syntax, quick startup (under 10ms), and good history search without needing a pile of plugins. Also a testbed for playing with job control and pipelines in a concurrent way. Not trying to be POSIX compatible, just less annoying than what I use now. Prototype first, polish later.
