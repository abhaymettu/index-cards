# Pebble-Lens

## Overview
Pebble-Lens is a small iOS app that lets you point your phone camera at a rock, pebble, or chunk of gravel and get a quick guess at what it is. Idea is on-device only, no network calls, so it works out on trails with no signal. Starting with a tiny set of common types (quartz, granite, basalt, sandstone) and a simple capture-then-classify flow. Want it to feel fast and a bit playful, not like a field guide. Biggest unknown is how well the model copes with wet or dirty stones. Keep scope small, ship something usable first, polish later.
